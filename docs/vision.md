# Vision

## Overview
OpenHealthOS aims to provide a secure, interoperable, and extensible platform for health data operations, service integration, and intelligent workflows.

## Goals
- Enable trusted exchange of health information.
- Support modular, scalable services.
- Reduce integration friction across clinical and operational systems.
- Provide a strong foundation for analytics, automation, and AI-assisted workflows.

## Long-Term Direction
The platform should remain developer-friendly while supporting enterprise-grade security, compliance, and operability.
