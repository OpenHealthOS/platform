# Mission

OpenHealthOS exists to create a dependable digital platform that helps teams build health technology solutions with clarity, safety, and speed. The mission is to align architecture, engineering practices, and product delivery around a shared set of principles that support patient impact and operational excellence.

## Core Commitments
- Deliver software that is secure by design.
- Build systems that are understandable and maintainable.
- Promote interoperability and standards-based integration.
- Support responsible innovation with strong governance.
