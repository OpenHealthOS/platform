## Purpose
This handbook captures the shared practices, conventions, and expectations for the OpenHealthOS platform engineering team. It is intentionally lightweight at the start and will grow as the platform matures.

## Working Principles
- Build in small, reviewable increments.
- Keep documentation close to the implementation.
- Favor clarity, testability, and maintainability over cleverness.
- Make architectural decisions explicit and searchable.

## Team Expectations
- Use the repository structure consistently.
- Keep changes scoped and easy to review.
- Document risky or non-obvious decisions.
- Maintain a healthy balance between feature delivery and platform quality.
